Privacy Policy — Barbell Load Calculator

Effective: October 14, 2026

Barbell Load Calculator ("the app") is developed by reiko51. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (units, bar weight, percentage range, theme, display options) are saved only on your device. Android may include them in your device backup if you have backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Optional tips

  • Google Play version: tips are processed entirely by Google Play. The developer never receives your payment details. Google's handling of the transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Version downloaded outside Google Play: the app can copy a Bitcoin address or PayPal email to your clipboard. Any payment happens in the wallet or PayPal app you choose, under that service's own privacy policy. The app itself sends nothing.

Permissions

The Google Play version includes Google Play Billing, which uses internet access only to talk to Google Play for tips. The version downloaded outside Google Play requests no permissions.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
